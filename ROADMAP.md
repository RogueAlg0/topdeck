# Roadmap

Where topdeck is headed, in plain terms. Dates are not promises; direction is.

## 0.1 — Every adapter, live

The first real milestone is a one-shot price lookup that works across all five games: Magic, Pokemon, Lorcana, One Piece, and Riftbound. The point of 0.1 is not features, it is proof: every price adapter returns real data from a free source, from the terminal, with no account and no key.

## 0.2–0.3 — Watch it daily

Once lookups work, topdeck starts remembering: daily price refreshes, history sparklines in the terminal, watch alerts when a card crosses your target, a market movers view for the day's biggest swings, and a portfolio that tracks what you own with profit and loss.

## 0.4–0.5 — Smarter buying

Pack expected value, so you can see what a sealed pack is really worth before you crack it. Sealed product prices alongside singles. A currency toggle for collectors outside the US. And a `topdeck doctor` command that checks every price source and tells you, honestly, which ones are healthy and which are down.

## 1.0 — Never pay full price for cardboard

The 1.0 promise is simple: never pay full price for cardboard. Fallback chains across sources so one outage never leaves you blind, trust tiers that tell you how much to believe each number, an offline-first SQLite store so your collection and history survive a dead connection, and a clean handoff to the private card scanner for the physical side of the hobby.

## What we never do

Some lines stay fixed, whatever the version:

- No telemetry. What you look up stays on your machine.
- No accounts. No signups, no logins, no lock-in.
- No paywalls. The CLI is free, and the roadmap is too.
- No buylist or graded prices we cannot source for free. If a number cannot be traced to a free source, it does not ship.

Anything beyond 1.0 lives on a private planning board, not here. This file covers the road we are building, not the map we are still drawing.
